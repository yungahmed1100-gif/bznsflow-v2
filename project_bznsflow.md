BznsFlow is a Done-For-You AI automation agency serving SMBs in Oman, Egypt, and Netherlands. 

**Why:** Helps businesses stop losing leads due to slow manual response times. Core pitch: "Your competitor just replied to your lead."

**How to apply:** The brand voice is direct, specific, and outcome-obsessed. Avoid generic SaaS platitudes. Always tie copy to real business pain (lost deals, no-shows, spreadsheet chaos). WhatsApp is the primary business channel for this market — center it prominently.

**Service tiers:**
- The Lead Saver: $15/mo — solo operators, instant response only
- The Control Center: $97/mo — clinics, real estate, salons — full CRM + booking
- The Scale Partner: $499/mo — enterprise, custom AI + Meta Ads management (3 clients/month cap)

**Tech stack (for credibility signals):** n8n, Groq, Supabase

**Markets:** Oman, Egypt, Netherlands — bilingual Arabic/English/Dutch by default, WhatsApp-first

**Key files:**
- src/translations.js — all copy (EN + AR)
- src/App.jsx — full React single-page site structure
- "Most Popular" badge on Tier 2 is hardcoded English in App.jsx (not in translations)
