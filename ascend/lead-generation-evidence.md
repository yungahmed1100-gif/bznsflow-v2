# Ascend lead-generation evidence register

Researched 2026-10-10 · Supports the [implementation proposal](lead-generation.md) and sector lead-generation sections.

## How to interpret this evidence

“Top performers” must name a population and a performance definition. ServiceTitan and Hinge provide explicit higher-performing cohorts; Zenoti publishes segment benchmarks but the full tables were not inspected here. The remaining references are industry surveys, named vendor cases, professional guidance or platform documentation. None establishes a verified best-performing Omani company in every sector, and observed association is not proof of causation.

Use the documented practice as a testable design input, then measure the business's own comparable cohorts. Do not import foreign CAC, margin, growth or conversion numbers as universal targets. Do not transfer a vendor case's reported uplift into an Ascend sales promise. Research and proposed design are distinct.

All sources below were accessed or searched on 2026-10-10. “Indexed excerpt only” is an explicit access limit. Undated living pages describe the inspected version. No private datasets, gated full reports or live ad accounts were accessed.

## L01

[ServiceTitan: top performers in the trades](https://www.servicetitan.com/toolbox/state-of-the-trades/trends/top-performer-profile)

**Evidence:** Higher-performing cohort; 12 months through January 2026.

Participating customers at the 90th revenue percentile within trade/market booked 62% of inbound lead calls versus 39% for others. The study also distinguishes structured options and service agreements. Correlation, vendor sample and residential-scope limits apply.

## L02

[Zenoti: 2026 Beauty and Wellness Benchmark overview](https://www.zenoti.com/blog/2026-beauty-wellness-benchmark-report)

**Evidence:** Segment benchmarks; North American 2025 operating data.

The public overview emphasizes memberships/retention and warns that high rebooking volume can conceal cancellations. Use completed repeat visits rather than calendar bookings as the growth outcome. Full percentile tables were not accessed.

## L03

[Hinge: 2025 High Growth Study](https://hingemarketing.com/blog/story/high-growth-study-2025-insights-into-todays-best-performing-firms)

**Evidence:** Higher-growth cohort; 770 professional-services firms.

High Growth means at least 20% compound annual growth over three years. The public findings emphasize educational content, differentiation and social visibility. This is cross-sector observational evidence; media, accounting, construction and technology adaptations below are proposed, not separate proven winners.

## L04

[Clio: 2025 solo and small law firms report release](https://www.clio.com/about/press/legal-trends-solo-small-law-firms-2025/)

**Evidence:** Vendor research; law-firm segment comparisons.

Referrals are prominent; firms combining them with digital intake tools show stronger reported outcomes. Use intake forms, scheduling and engagement progression. Association does not establish that the tools alone caused growth.

## L05

[NAR: 2025 Home Buyers and Sellers Generational Trends](https://www.nar.realtor/sites/default/files/2025-04/2025-home-buyers-and-sellers-generational-trends-04-01-2025.pdf)

**Evidence:** US buyer/seller survey; exhibits 4-4 and seller-agent acquisition.

The agent-discovery tables distinguish referrals, previous agents and property-led inquiries. This supports tracking relationship and property sources separately; it is not a ranking of top Omani agencies.

## L06

[ADA: attracting new patients](https://www.ada.org/resources/practice/practice-management/attracting-new-patients-to-your-dental-practice)

**Evidence:** Professional association guidance.

Practice/service information and clear website presentation support patient acquisition. Apply to administrative discovery and booking; this is guidance, not a top-decile growth benchmark or treatment recommendation.

## L07

[Zocdoc: 2025 What Patients Want release](https://www.zocdoc.com/about/news/zocdoc-reveals-evolving-patient-preferences-and-trends-in-third-annual-what-patients-want-report/)

**Evidence:** US booking-platform observations.

The report describes patients seeking accessible appointment times and cost-conscious care. Use real availability and administrative matching. Marketplace behavior is not proof of an Oman-wide acquisition uplift.

## L08

[Mindbody: 2025 State of the Industry release](https://www.mindbodyonline.com/company/press/fitness-wellness-industry-overwhelmingly-optimistic-about-performance-mindbodys-2025-state)

**Evidence:** Operator survey.

Respondents identify Instagram acquisition and personalized retention outreach. Test those channels locally and measure attended trials and continuing memberships; this is self-reported sector evidence, not causal top-performer proof.

## L09

[Tekmetric: digital vehicle inspections](https://tours.tekmetric.com/answers/digital-vehicle-inspection-software-and-mobile-app)

**Evidence:** Vendor workflow guidance.

Documented findings, visual explanations and saved declined work support informed estimate decisions and follow-up. This source describes a workflow; it does not establish a universal repair-shop conversion benchmark.

## L10

[SevenRooms: 2025 UAE restaurant trends](https://sevenrooms.com/research/2025-UAE-restaurant-trends/)

**Evidence:** Regional consumer/operator research and platform observations.

The report connects social/Google discovery, booking convenience and personalized contact. Some campaign claims use global data and may estimate spend; no quoted revenue multiplier is adopted as an Ascend target.

## L11

[Shopify: Stellar Eats checkout case](https://www.shopify.com/uk/blog/stellar-eats)

**Evidence:** Named vendor case study; consumer packaged food.

A simplified checkout reduced purchase friction in this seller's reported experience. Apply transparent totals and short checkout as hypotheses for retail/food; this is not evidence about all custom cake businesses.

## L12

[RepairDesk: Mr. Phone Pro](https://blog.repairdesk.co/2026/02/13/how-mr-phone-pro-turned-missed-calls-into-booked-repairs-with-repairdesk/)

**Evidence:** Named vendor case; February 2026; indexed excerpt only.

The available indexed excerpt describes structured lead handling and follow-through for price shoppers. Full-page retrieval failed; no numerical uplift or universal ranking is adopted. Voice/missed-call features require a separate connector and are not current Ascend capabilities.

## L13

[HubSpot: University of Wyoming](https://www.hubspot.com/case-studies/university-of-wyoming)

**Evidence:** Named education vendor case.

The case reports an increase in lead volume. Ascend should test program-specific intake against enrollment and attendance, not transfer the headline result to training SMEs or count leads as enrolled learners.

## L14

[HubSpot: Ekol Logistics](https://www.hubspot.com/case-studies/ekol-logistics)

**Evidence:** Named logistics vendor case.

The case describes shared records, automatic website lead capture and direct routing to the appropriate salesperson. It supports a workflow pattern, not a guaranteed sales uplift or proof that all shipping SMEs need HubSpot.

## L15

[SiteMinder: Hotel Booking Trends](https://www.siteminder.com/hotel-booking-trends/)

**Evidence:** 2025 booking-platform data across 20 markets.

The report supports balanced distribution alongside a usable direct booking experience. Direct-booking values differ with stay/room mix; averages are not causal uplift or a matched Omani hotel benchmark. Hotel evidence does not automatically apply to travel agencies.

## L16

[Cvent: 2025 Europe Planner Sourcing Report release](https://www.cvent.com/en/press-release/latest-cvent-planner-sourcing-report-reveals-anticipated-2025-person-event-growth)

**Evidence:** Planner survey; November 2024 release.

Planners want timely, customized venue responses. Adapt to a complete brief and source-backed proposal workflow; European venue sourcing is a proxy for event-service acquisition, not proof for every wedding or production company.

[Thomas: 2024 annual sourcing snapshot](https://business.thomasnet.com/2024-annual-sourcing)

**Evidence:** Industrial sourcing activity; public summary.

Category-level sourcing activity supports capability-specific industrial discovery. The public page is not a top-manufacturer lead-conversion study; detailed RFQ qualification below is an engineering proposal.

## L18

[Jobber: 2026 Home Service Trends](https://www.getjobber.com/home-service-trends-report/)

**Evidence:** Survey of 1,050 US home-service owners; December 2025.

The report includes cleaning and general contracting, with referrals/repeat work and sales/scheduling themes. Findings are self-reported and do not establish Omani channel costs or target profit margins.

## P01

[Google Business Profile: local ranking guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)

**Evidence:** Official platform guidance; accessed 2026-10-10.

Maintain accurate business/service information and useful local presence. Eligibility and supported features need checking for the business and market; no ranking guarantee is implied.

## P02

[Google Ads: qualified and converted leads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/11459091?hl=en)

**Evidence:** Official measurement guidance; accessed 2026-10-10.

Define and send appropriate downstream outcomes where permitted. A qualified lead, confirmed appointment and sale are different events; choose the actual business outcome for each supported campaign.

## P03

[Google Ads: customer data policies](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/7475709?hl=en)

**Evidence:** Official policy; accessed 2026-10-10.

Sensitive-category conversions cannot be used in enhanced conversions or store-sales uploads. Export requires an explicit destination/purpose policy; hashing or renaming the event does not make prohibited information eligible.

[Meta: lead-generation forms guide](https://about.fb.com/ltam/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2023/11/LeadGenerationGuide.pdf)

**Evidence:** Official 2023 guide; indexed excerpt only.

The indexed excerpt describes CRM feedback through Conversions API for conversion-leads optimization. The PDF exceeded the fetch limit; current endpoint, permissions and supported event requirements remain implementation verification work.


## Coverage and unresolved evidence

The 25 playbooks explicitly map to these sources. Beauty/trades/professional services have stronger comparative evidence than custom cakes, media production and the Other fallback. Cafés borrow relevant restaurant/commerce findings; education uses a university case as a limited proxy for training SMEs; hospitality observations cannot establish travel-agency results.

Before describing any pack as “proven for top performers in Oman,” obtain dated local channel spend, qualified populations, verified attendance/fulfillment, paid/returned outcomes and cost coverage. Match the business subtype and sales cycle. Until then, use “evidence-informed proposed workflow.”

Meta's detailed incoming-message reference returned a rate-limit error during inspection; the lead-form guide was available only through indexed excerpts because its PDF exceeded the fetch limit. Verify current official API schemas, permissions and event eligibility during implementation. No unsupported endpoint or automatic campaign connection is certified here.
