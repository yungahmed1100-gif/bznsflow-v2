## Applying “best-performing” evidence

Use percentile comparisons only when the source actually publishes them and the business population, metric formula, period, size and operating model match. Tekmetric's public page offers average/top-25% comparisons; those are North American shop observations. Zenoti's public overview describes segment percentile benchmarks without providing the full tables used here. AICPA's detailed comparison platform is member-only. No inaccessible percentile value was supplied as an Ascend default.

HFA's published retention figure is an average, not top-quartile. Clio's published legal-practice figures are averages. Neither becomes a target simply because it appears in an industry report. The dental/IHI and other older methodology references support measurement concepts, not current commercial benchmarks.

SCOR, DORA, OEE and evaluation frameworks support definitions and balanced measurement. Workflow guidance supports practical design choices. None proves that deploying these dashboard features will produce a stated financial uplift.

Public source excerpts and available pages were reviewed; full gated reports were not purchased or claimed as read. The hotel glossary and Clio benchmark page had direct-fetch failures; indexed official excerpts supplied the cited definitions/context. No benchmark figure depends on pretending those failures were successful full-page reads.

The formulas in the sector specs are the proposed local measurement contract. Where a formula is deliberately customized—for example a matured cohort or a specific cancellation policy—it must not be labeled as the publisher's exact benchmark definition until reconciled.
